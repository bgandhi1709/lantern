# Book content comes only from its rights holder; a Board is a Family setting

Lantern is a technology that ingests and explains Books; it owns none of them. Each source is taken only from whoever holds its rights, who stays responsible for it: a government publisher's own site (NCERT for CBSE, ebalbharati for SSC), or a school under an agreement in which the school states it holds the rights to what it shares. Third-party PDF mirrors are never a source, however complete. This is why ICSE waits: CISCE prescribes no Book, schools pick commercial publishers (Selina, Frank, Morning Star…), and the only bulk source found (studiestoday) shows no licence, so ICSE content arrives when a school brings its own.

A Family follows one Board, picked at registration and fixed for the pilot; every Child in it follows the same Board. Mixed Families (siblings on different Boards) are not served, and changing Board means a Family delete and registering again, so no Plan, Check or History ever has to move between Boards. Considered and rejected: Board on the Child (handles mixed Families, but every Plan, Check and Answer lookup would carry the Board as well as the Class) and an editable Board (needs History entries tagged by Board, as Class changes are).
